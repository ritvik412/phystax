Bring your own experiment.

Toss, drop, swing or roll something in front of a still camera. SAM 3.1 tracks it from one tap. Muse Spark sees the first half of the motion, decides what kind of motion it is, and seals a prediction for the second half before reading it. Then the video shows what really happened.

Recording

Allow camera access to begin. Prop the camera so it does not move; a plain background and good light help. The object must move on its own: release it, do not carry it.
